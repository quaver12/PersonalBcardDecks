h:The Sigma rules: ABCDEF - Assume NΣi=1(x) where N is the upper limit, Σ is sigma, i=1 is the lower limit, and (x) is the formula for the terms
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
q:How can you rewrite the following? NΣi=1(i)
a:(N^2+N)/2
 - Rule 'D' - "Divi-2" it's the one equation that gets divided by 2
q:How can you rewrite the following? NΣi=1(1)
a:N
 - Rule 'C' - "Constant" self explanatory - just becomes a constant = to upper limit

q:How can you rewrite the following? bΣi=a(1)
a:b-a+1
 - Rule 'B' - Battle - equation looks like the word battle

q:How can you rewrite the following? bΣi=1(M) - assuming M is independant of i (copy-paste formula to make typing it easier)
a:M(bΣi=a(1))
 - or M(b-a+1) - Rule 'E' - "Escape" - the M got out of the equation

q:How can you rewrite the following? bΣi=a(A*i) (copy-paste formula to make typing it easier)
a:A*(bΣi=a(i))
 - Rule 'F' - "Forgetful" - the A escaped, but forgot the i

q:How can you rewrite the following? bΣi=a(Ai+B)
a:(bΣi=a(Ai))+(bΣi=a(B))
 - Rule 'A' - "Asexual reproduction - mitosis" - one equation separates into 2
